# Chapter 1: Introduction to MLflow for GenAI on Databricks

This chapter sets the foundation: what MLflow is, why it matters for generative AI
work on Databricks, and the running example the rest of the book builds on — a
customer-service assistant for the fictional airline Unity Airways.

## In this chapter
- What Is MLflow?
- Why MLflow for GenAI?
- Introducing the Book's Core Use Case
- Conclusion and Key Takeaways

## Key takeaways
- MLflow brings structure, reproducibility, and governance to the ML and GenAI
  lifecycle, addressing the usual pain points of ad hoc experiment tracking and
  unmanaged models in production.
- GenAI applications are more complex than traditional ML and demand disciplined
  practices; MLflow 3.x adds tracing, evaluation, and versioning features aimed at that.
- The Unity Airways assistant helps customers with bookings, rescheduling,
  cancellations, and policy questions, drawing on both structured booking records
  and unstructured FAQ documents.
- The goal of the use case is to demonstrate MLflow best practices on Databricks
  rather than the business value itself.

This is a conceptual chapter with no notebooks; its material is covered in full in the book.
